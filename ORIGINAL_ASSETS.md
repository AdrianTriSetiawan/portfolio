# 3D Asset Provenance & Rollback

## Worawork-style player source

`public/models/Player.glb` is the clean, pre-existing Worawork-style character
asset. It is loaded directly at runtime; the binary is **not edited or
resaved**. Its authored glTF bounds are:

- min `[-1.4746, 0.0000, -1.0061]`
- max `[1.4746, 3.5902, 1.0061]`
- unscaled size `2.9491 W × 3.5902 H × 2.0122 D`

`CleanWorld.jsx` applies a non-destructive runtime material clone (warm cream
lighting/fabric tint) and adds indigo haori/obi trim plus a small sakura hair
pin. The source mesh, UVs, textures, skin, glasses, face, silhouette, and
13 authored animation clips remain intact. The runtime scale is `0.90` on
all axes, yielding a measured `3.2312` world-unit avatar height; the gameplay
collider radius is `0.50` world units and is intentionally not reduced for the
visual scale.

This is a runtime restyle, not a claim that the source character or model was
authored by this project. No Hunyuan3D asset is used, and
`OriginalAvatar.glb` is not loaded by the active scene.

## Other Japanese scene assets

`OriginalRyokan.glb` and `OriginalSakuraTree.glb` are procedural project
derivatives generated by `scripts/generate_original_assets.js`. The original
tree GLB is preserved but is not used for visible trees because its
low-scale dodecahedron clusters read as pink blobs in the diorama. The active
`CleanWorld.jsx` builds three low-poly sakura trees from dark tapered trunks,
branching limbs, separated layered pink blossom clusters, and the animated
falling-petal system. The legacy `Models.glb` cottage and foliage meshes are
hidden where Japanese scene assets replace them.

The foreground sign has no separate node. Its surface and lettering are baked
into `Land_lp_Bake1_CyclesBake S2A_COMBINED` (image 13), which the active
`Land_lp` mesh's `Land` material references. The earlier lettering patch was
mistakenly painted as a rectangle into image 2, the night atlas; it left the
visible daytime `WW'S` lettering untouched. That patch is removed. Both day
and night atlas lettering now replace the original glyph pixels in-place with
`ADRIAN'S`, keeping the board's wood grain and cream painted style. The active
land atlas shifts lime grass toward moss green and turquoise toward jade while
preserving its baked texture detail. No geometry, UVs, or runtime sign overlay
were added.

The active runtime sakura trees sit on dry lawn at `[-7.8, 1.4, 0.8]`,
`[-4.8, 1.4, 1.8]`, and `[-8.5, 1.4, -2.5]`. Their collision circles follow
the trunks and avoid the pond surface, house footprint, and hidden `Trees_lp`
geometry.

## Asset rollback

Clean player backups are preserved at:

- `.asset-backup-20260930_101912/models/Player.glb`
- `.asset-backup-20260930_142600/public/models/Player.glb`
- `.asset-backup-20260930_142600/models-Player-clean.glb`

To roll back the runtime source, restore one of those files to
`public/models/Player.glb`; no generated derivative needs to be removed.
